What the screen shows for an explained alert

Decided 24 October 2026: evidence panel, one line per hop, counts first, stacked steps that collapse after. Citation letters are L (log), G (graph), D (document). The buttons still switch to the options not taken. The Investigator chat after the user presses Investigate on the S6 alert (the spray that worked). Second screen: the benign twin B1, where the answer is "nothing to see, here is why". Third screen: the one small change on the Alerts panel. Switch with the bar below or the arrow keys.

Decision 1. Where a citation lands

  • The answer says "mgarcia is in IT Admins [G1]". The label is plain text from the agent; code swaps it for the real record or passage after the answer. Something must show what A1 is.
  • Footnotes: a numbered list under the answer, one line per citation: source, ID, first line of the passage. Read like a paper.
  • Expandable chips: click [G1] in the sentence and the passage opens right there, under the sentence. Nothing moves away from the text.
  • Evidence panel: a third column on the right lists every cited record and passage for the whole conversation, built from the saved artifact. Clicking [G1] highlights its row. The panel is the eval's artifact made visible.

Decision 2. How a graph path is written as plain text

  • The graph says: account mgarcia, member of IT Admins, which is granted the domain controller, which is restricted. No drawing is allowed, because graph fields never pass through a markup renderer.
  • One line per hop: an indented list, each hop a short sentence with its record ID.
  • Arrow chain: one monospace line, mgarcia → IT Admins → domain controller (restricted). Compact, reads like a path.
  • Hop table: columns From, Relation, To, Record. The most literal, the heaviest.

Decision 3. How the three radii are shown

  • Ticket 04 fixed three radii: groups held, assets reachable, peers by shared device and network. S6's peers can be a dozen names.
  • Counts first: three small tiles in the answer with the number and the worst item; the full lists sit in Tools used (or the evidence panel). The chat stays readable.
  • Full tables: three tables inside the answer. Everything visible at once, the chat gets long.

Decision 4. What the user sees while the playbook runs, and after

  • The investigation tool runs five internal steps and streams progress per step (ticket 05, decision 2). The Port already shows a "Tools used" drawer under each answer.
  • Collapse after: the step list stacks live while the investigation tool runs (an exception to the Port's single status line, for this tool only), then folds into the Tools used drawer once the answer arrives. Open the drawer to see it again.
  • Stay visible: the step list stays above the answer as a permanent receipt of what was checked.

What is fixed already, from tickets 04 to 06

  • The chat is the screen. No context section on the Alerts panel. Nothing runs unless asked.
  • Labels like [G1] are typed by the agent; code resolves them to real IDs and passages after the answer. An unresolvable label gets one retry, then shows as [D9?] with a warning "one citation could not be verified".
  • Document text, graph values and log values show as plain text only. The answer prose is the one markdown boundary, as in the Port, with links and images off.
  • Everything on this screen comes from the saved artifact, which is also what the eval reads. Screen and exam see the same payload.
  • The faithfulness judge's reason is not shown. It is a smoke alarm for the eval, not analyst information.

The other half of trust: an alert that is cleared

  • B1 is a sales user signing in from Germany for three days. The Port's new-country rule flags it. A travel notice clears it.
  • The same shape as the S6 answer, so the user learns one layout. The difference is the verdict and the "cleared by" document, which gets its own line and its own citation.
  • The report card turns green and says benign. The radii still show, because "what could it have reached" matters even when nothing happened.
  • Drawn with the chosen set: evidence panel, one line per hop, counts first, collapse after.
