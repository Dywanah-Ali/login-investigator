The agent does not close alerts, change accounts or policies, initiate responses, expand inventory, or adopt either vendor’s workflow.\n\n**Competing hypotheses.** Decompose the investigation into checks rather than one compound question:\n\n- What happened in the authentication sequence: successes, failures, MFA steps and applied policy?\n- Is the device, application, IP or geography familiar for this account?\n- Is there a time-valid, applicable MFA exception or approved journey?\n- Does threat guidance support a malicious interpretation?\n\nCompare at least **compromise**, **legitimate activity**, and **insufficient evidence**. CORTEX’s agents shared a tool library with its single-agent baseline; its results concern a bundled workflow, not this tool-interface question.
