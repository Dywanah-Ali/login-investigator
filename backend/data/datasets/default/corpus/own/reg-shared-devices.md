## Use and responsibility

The meeting-room PC is available to its listed users for meeting preparation and presentation work. Leave the desk clear and end the account session after use. Do not assume the next person is allowed to view the information left on screen. Report equipment faults to the helpdesk using the device name.

The warehouse terminal supports work in the warehouse area. The same individual-account rule applies even when users change over quickly. A person's presence in this table does not grant access to another person's work or increase their system permissions. Access continues to depend on their own approved groups.

## Review arrangements

IT Support reviews this register twice a year. The next routine review is due in December 2026. Confirm the physical location, the people who still need the machine and any change in how it is used. Location changes and requests to add or remove users should be sent to IT Support when they arise.

For a sign-in enquiry, check the actual session time and account alongside this register. The user list describes expected use; it is not a record of who used the device at a particular moment. Ask the relevant team to clarify a session when that detail is needed.
