# Self-Service Password Reset Is Coming

## Message

From: Joseph Burton (joseph.burton), IT Support, joseph.burton@corp.example

To: All Staff

Sent: 2026-09-28 at 13:00 UTC

Subject: Self-service password reset arrives on 2026-10-19

## What is changing

A self-service password reset portal on the intranet is scheduled to arrive on 2026-10-19. It will guide you through the required identity checks and password reset steps. We will provide the opening instructions through the normal staff guidance when it is ready.

Until then, all password resets go through the helpdesk. This message announces a future change; it does not mean the self-service process is available today. If you have forgotten your password, contact the helpdesk and let the analyst verify your identity before a reset.

## What stays the same

Your new password must still have at least 14 characters and must be unique. A passphrase of unrelated words can be easier to remember. You must not share the password or give anyone your authenticator or recovery codes.

Please open the intranet through your normal company route to read launch guidance. Do not enter a password after clicking a link in an email claiming to offer the new service. The helpdesk will remain available for questions and for people who cannot complete the self-service steps. We will send an update if the planned launch date changes.
