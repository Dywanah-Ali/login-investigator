# MFA Becomes Mandatory on 2 March

## Message

From: Thomas Price (thomas.price), Security, thomas.price@corp.example

To: All Staff

Sent: 2026-02-16 at 10:00 UTC

Subject: MFA becomes mandatory on 2026-03-02

## Please set up your authenticator app

From 2026-03-02, every company account must use multi-factor authentication, or MFA. This adds an authenticator app check to the password you already use. The requirement applies in both offices and when working remotely.

Please allow time to enrol before that date. Open the intranet through your normal company route and follow the authenticator app installation instructions. Start enrolment from the identity provider, pair the app as directed and complete a test sign-in that you initiate yourself. If anything is unclear, contact the helpdesk before continuing.

During setup, generate the authenticator's one-time recovery codes. Keep them private in a secure place separate from the device used for the app. They are the fallback if the app cannot be used. Each code works once. Do not email the codes to yourself or send them to support.

Only approve an MFA request for a sign-in you have just started. An unexpected request should be reported to Security, even if it stops before you respond. Nobody in IT needs you to approve a request on their behalf.

## Help with enrolment

The helpdesk can guide you through installation and testing. It will not ask for your password or a recovery code. Please raise setup problems early rather than waiting until the requirement takes effect.

Security must approve and record any exception under the MFA policy. A setup difficulty does not automatically permit sign-in without MFA. Thank you for completing the enrolment and test before 2026-03-02.
