# Helpdesk Opening Hours Extended

## Message

From: Joseph Burton (joseph.burton), IT Support, joseph.burton@corp.example

To: All Staff

Sent: 2026-09-10 at 11:00 UTC

Subject: Longer helpdesk phone hours from 2026-09-14

## New opening hours

From 2026-09-14, the helpdesk phone line will open from 06:00 to 22:00 UTC every day of the week. This includes weekends. The hours are expressed in UTC for staff in both the UK office and the US office, so please check what they mean for your local working day.

Use the existing helpdesk phone contact details. You do not need a different number for the extended hours. The longer opening period is intended to make it easier to get help when starting early, working late or working across offices.

## Before calling

Have your company laptop name and a short description of the problem ready. Tell the analyst when the problem started, using UTC where possible. If you already have an SD- ticket reference, give that reference so the analyst can continue the same request.

Password resets still require identity verification. Please do not send passwords, authenticator codes or recovery codes in advance. The analyst will explain the checks and guide you through the next steps. These requirements apply throughout the extended opening hours.
